About Me

Abi Arevalo

I am an architecture student with a strong interest in architectural design, urban planning, and spatial thinking. I enjoy exploring how architecture shapes environments, supports communities, and responds to human needs.

My work focuses on conceptual development, program planning, environmental analysis, and the creation of meaningful spatial experiences that combine creativity, functionality, and context.

Through academic projects and professional experience, I have developed a growing interest in planning processes, site analysis, coordination, and the relationship between design decisions and project execution.
